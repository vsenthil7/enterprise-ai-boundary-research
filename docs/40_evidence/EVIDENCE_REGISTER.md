# Evidence Register

This register tracks evidence used to support or challenge research claims.

| Evidence ID | Date added | Domain | Claim supported/challenged | Organisation / source | Evidence type | Evidence level | Measured outcome? | Counterevidence noted? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules

1. Named deployments do not automatically prove economic value.
2. Vendor and consultancy material remains E2 unless corroborated.
3. Measured production evidence is preferred over announcements and pilots.
4. Every important positive AI claim should have a counterevidence search.
5. Record source date and distinguish current evidence from stale evidence.
