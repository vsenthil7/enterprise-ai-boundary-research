# Banking — Research Pass 001

**Date:** 05/10/2026
**Status:** Evidence collection started; not final conclusion.

## Initial evidence

### Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance — 2026 Global AI in Financial Services Report

Survey evidence (339 respondents represented in the agentic maturity comparison):

- Traditional financial institutions: 23% deployed agentic AI, 24% piloting, 53% not yet piloting/deploying.
- FinTechs: 25% deployed, 33% piloting, 42% not yet piloting/deploying.
- Advanced economies: 21% deployed, 30% piloting, 49% not yet piloting/deploying.
- AI-mature firms: 34% deployed vs 17% among less-mature firms.

**Interpretation:** agentic AI is real but is not yet the dominant operating model across financial services. This is counterevidence to blanket claims that banking has already become agentic.

Evidence level: E3 for adoption survey; confidence 90% pending methodology review.

### Evident AI Outcomes Report 2025

Across 50 major banks, Evident reported that only 9 had documented an agentic AI use case in production or pilot stage. Reported outcomes were concentrated in front office and IT/security.

**Interpretation:** as of the report period, production/pilot evidence was concentrated rather than universal.

Evidence level: E2/E3 depending on underlying bank disclosure; confidence 85%.

### Lloyds Banking Group — direct corporate disclosure

Lloyds reported approximately £50m of GenAI-generated value in 2025, 57 GenAI use cases in production, 30,000 customer-facing colleagues using its knowledge-management tool, 4,000 working days saved by an HR solution, and a commercial-real-estate lending process taking 5 minutes using agentic AI. It expected more than £100m additional value in 2026.

**Interpretation:** strong evidence that AI can create measurable value around employee knowledge, software engineering, fraud, HR and lending workflows. It does not establish that deterministic account/transaction systems should be replaced.

Evidence level: E3/E4 (direct company disclosure; measurement methodology still needs scrutiny); confidence 90% on reported figures, lower on causal attribution.

### Nubank production research

A 2026 paper reports five production customer-support deployments. In card delivery, large-scale A/B testing reported +37 percentage points in AI transactional NPS and +29 percentage points in self-service rate versus prior agent variants; AI satisfaction on most use cases approached expert-human levels.

**Interpretation:** strong production evidence for LLM/agent value in customer-support interpretation and self-service. This is not evidence for replacing authoritative record or transaction engines.

Evidence level: E4 candidate; confidence 90% pending full-paper methodological review.

## Boundary finding — provisional

Current evidence supports a layered model:

- authoritative records, transactions and fixed calculations: deterministic core remains necessary;
- customer/employee knowledge interpretation: demonstrated LLM value;
- variable lending/compliance/support workflows: emerging agent value;
- universal replacement of banking applications/core systems: not supported by evidence collected so far.

## Mandatory ordinary-software test

Static balance lookup, statement retrieval, known payment execution and repeatable calculations still fail the case for an agent unless an additional cognitive or variable-workflow problem is present.
