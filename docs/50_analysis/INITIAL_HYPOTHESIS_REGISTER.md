# Initial Hypothesis Register

These are hypotheses to test, not conclusions.

| ID | Hypothesis | Initial confidence | Evidence level | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| H-001 | Deterministic systems of record and transaction engines will remain the authoritative core for most enterprise workloads. | 95% | E1 | OPEN |
| H-002 | The largest enterprise impact of agentic AI will be on human cognitive/glue work around systems, exceptions, diagnosis, interpretation and variable cross-system work rather than on replacing databases or transaction engines. | 70% | E1 | OPEN |
| H-003 | Production DWH/reporting execution remains largely deterministic even if AI materially changes design, build, support, diagnosis and commentary work. | 85% | E1 | OPEN |
| H-004 | Fixed BAU reports, scheduled ETL, static retrieval and known workflows usually do not justify an agent unless a distinct cognitive/variable-workflow problem exists. | 90% | E1 | OPEN |
| H-005 | A meaningful share of AI/agent proposals will fail the 'WHY NOT ORDINARY SOFTWARE?' test once economics, risk and existing automation are examined. | 80% | E1 | OPEN |

## Rules

- Do not silently promote a hypothesis into a fact.
- Update confidence only when evidence is added.
- Record counterevidence.
- Preserve superseded values in history rather than rewriting the past without explanation.
